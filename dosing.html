Reading mg, mL and syringe U: 1 mg = 1,000 mcg. Concentration = vial mass (mg) ÷ final solution volume (mL). Volume (mL) = amount (mg) ÷ concentration (mg/mL). For a U-100 syringe only, U = mL × 100; 0.1 mL = 10 U. These are volume markings, not peptide international units. Tables use the catalog’s selectable vial sizes, rather than COA sample strengths. Reconstitution amounts are fixed to the linked source for each matching vial strength. Conversions use that specific concentration. Additional sizes use calculator-checked volumes chosen to match the linked reference concentration and are labeled “Calculated.” These mathematical extensions do not establish product-specific solubility, diluent compatibility, stability, or container capacity. Some calculated volumes exceed 3 mL; confirm the actual product’s preparation requirements and vial capacity before treating them as mixing instructions. Figures are approximate: do not round a small volume to a syringe marking it cannot measure. A row exceeding one vial or 100 U is flagged. Nasal reference schedules use mL without syringe U.

Published Human Research

Research Feature Study Description
Participants Healthy adult volunteers
Administration Single ascending doses and repeated weekly or biweekly dosing
Measures Growth hormone, IGF-1, pharmacokinetics, and tolerability

The foundational clinical study evaluated weight-based research doses, including 30 and 60 micrograms/kg. Those study doses do not establish a general-purpose bodybuilding or wellness schedule.

Key Considerations

  • Growth hormone and IGF-1 changes are biological measures, not proof of improved performance.
  • Repeated dosing can produce cumulative effects on IGF-1.
